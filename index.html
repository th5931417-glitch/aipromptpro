Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

What we store

When you sign in, your email address is saved only in your own browser (local storage) so the site can remember you. We do not run a server database of accounts on this site. Signing out removes it from your browser.

Your text entries

The topic or details you type to customize a prompt stay in your browser. We do not save or send them to us. They are shared only when you tap Run, which opens ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini with your finished prompt.

Third-party services

Payments are handled by Dodo Payments on their own checkout page; we do not see or store your card details. ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini are run by other companies and handle your prompts under their own privacy policies.

Your control

Use Sign out in the Account menu to remove your email from this browser. You can also clear this site's data in your browser settings at any time.
